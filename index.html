Learning Tigrinya? Start by hearing it.

A translator built for how people actually learn: a starter phrasebook of everyday Tigrinya and Amharic, a speaker button on every phrase so you can hear it, and a voice typer so you can practice saying things and see them written down. Made for the diaspora kids, the partners, the grandkids — anyone trying to hold a real conversation with family.

📖 Every entry below is a starter entry — the basics: greetings, thanks, yes/no, a few everyday words. Double-check spellings with a native speaker before you use them anywhere formal. Expanding and correcting this list with family is honestly part of the project.
